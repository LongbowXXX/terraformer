# CRITICAL PROTOCOLS (ABSOLUTE PRIORITY)

The following rules must be adhered to as the absolute priority in all situations:

1.  **Artifact Localization**:

    - Artifacts presented to the user (Implementation Plans, Request Checklists, Verification Items) must always be written in **Japanese**.

2.  **Language Standards**:
    - **Thinking Process**: Must be conducted in **English**.
    - **User Explanations/Responses**: Must be written in **Japanese**. (System errors and log citations may be kept in their original text).
    - **Repository Files**: Must always be written in **English**.
